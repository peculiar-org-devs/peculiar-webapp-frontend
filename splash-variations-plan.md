# Splash Screen Variations Plan

Present these three distinct splash screen variations to your design team. Each leverages simple, code-friendly animations (Scale, Slide, Fade) and interprets the brand's journey from "vibrant and playful" to "clean and polished" in a unique way.

---

## Variation 1: The Vibrant Pulse (Energy & Tech)
- **Background:** Deep Royal Purple (#3A2256) for a premium, vibrant tech feel.
- **Hook:** Gradient purple "P" symbol pops into center, scaling from 0% to 100% with a playful spring/bounce effect.
- **Reveal:** "P" slides left, "eculiar" wordmark in Champagne (#F7E6CA) fades in next to it.
- **Climax:** Tagline "Your Event, Perfected" fades in below logo in Satoshi font.
- **Transition:** Smooth crossfade to pure White (#FFFFFF) before onboarding slides.
- **Why it works:** Immediate excitement with gradient logo, slide and white-out transition reassure user of platform's solid structure.

---

## Variation 2: The Elegant Unveil (Quiet Luxury)
- **Background:** Champagne (#F7E6CA) signals value and premium experience.
- **Hook:** Deep Royal Purple "P" symbol gracefully fades in, slowly scales from 90% to 100% (no bounce).
- **Reveal:** Gentle slide left reveals purple "eculiar" wordmark with mask reveal effect.
- **Climax:** Tagline fades in using deep purple Satoshi font.
- **Transition:** Swift upward swipe to White (#FFFFFF) background, leading to onboarding.
- **Why it works:** Sophisticated and polished, mimics high-end event planning experience.

---

## Variation 3: The Pattern Focus (Arty Brand Identity)
- **Background:** Deep Royal Purple (#3A2256) with faint Peculiar brand pattern (repeating "P" shapes) panning diagonally.
- **Hook:** Dynamic, arty vibe with moving pattern.
- **Reveal:** Pattern blurs/fades out, full Champagne logo lockup scales up to 100%.
- **Climax:** Tagline elegantly fades in below.
- **Transition:** Screen fades to White (#FFFFFF) for stress-free onboarding.
- **Why it works:** Utilizes brand assets, creates depth and a memorable, fun branded moment.

---

**Tips for Design Presentation:**
- All three options are easily executable in your current tech stack (React, Tailwind, Framer Motion).
- Use standard CSS properties: `transform: scale`, `transform: translateX`, `opacity`.
- Animations can be orchestrated with Framer Motion for smooth transitions.
